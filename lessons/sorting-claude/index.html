A course in reasoning about algorithms

Sorting

Sorting is the acquisition, representation, maintenance and exploitation of information about order.

This is one long chain of reasoning, not a collection of tutorials. Each lesson opens from the question the previous one left unresolved, and nothing is used before it has been built. Read them in order; they are written to be read that way and will feel strange otherwise.

The goal is not to memorise sorting algorithms. It is to be able to meet an unfamiliar algorithmic problem and reason about it from first principles. Sorting is the vehicle.
