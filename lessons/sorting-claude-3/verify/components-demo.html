Q5

Arrange the proof of the lemma and exclude the false step.

  1. Assume \(a \prec b\) and \(b \prec a\).
  2. Transitivity gives \(a \prec a\).
  3. By totality, one of them must fail.
  4. This contradicts irreflexivity.
That is the proof.
Start from the assumption.

Assume, apply transitivity, contradict irreflexivity.
