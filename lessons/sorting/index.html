Advanced Computer Science Monograph Series

The Theory of Sorting

"Sorting is fundamentally about acquiring, representing, maintaining, and exploiting information about order. This monograph derives the subject from first principles across discrete mathematics, information theory, computational complexity, and physical hardware constraints."
Curriculum Architecture & Epistemological Spine

This course is structured as an interconnected mathematical derivation. Rather than studying algorithms in isolation, each chapter emerges naturally from the limitations, questions, and invariants established by its predecessors:

$$\text{Ordering} \longrightarrow \text{Permutations} \longrightarrow \text{Information} \longrightarrow \text{Decision Trees} \longrightarrow \Omega(n \log n) \text{ Lower Bound}$$ $$\text{Observation} \longrightarrow \text{Invariant Discovery} \longrightarrow \text{Progress Metric} \longrightarrow \text{Proof of Correctness} \longrightarrow \text{Complexity}$$
